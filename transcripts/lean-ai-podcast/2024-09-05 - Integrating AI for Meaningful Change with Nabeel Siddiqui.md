So what we usually do is, before we get into actual development cycle, we create these small experiments with focus groups where we have end users of representing different functions. [00:04:44] Let's say we present them with option A of a mock-up and say that, hey, do you like this or you like option B? We run it through a formal A-B test experiment, but what we get out of as a result is a validation that, hey, option A is meeting the needs of the user. Why we don't ask first is sometimes because in the world of AI, we are ahead in terms of users' requirement. So we need to show, we need to present something before we form an opinion on that. So we don't form an opinion, we observe, we let the users talk, choose the options, and then we move forward.
